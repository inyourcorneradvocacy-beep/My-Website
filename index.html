I've sat on every side of the table — and I know what it feels like to walk in hoping someone is in your corner.

I'm a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), educational consultant, former special education teacher, and literacy expert. That combination means I can help you understand not just the paperwork, but the behavior plans, academic goals, and everyday decisions behind your child's IEP.

I know how overwhelming it can feel to sit across from a team of professionals while trying to advocate for your child — because I've been the parent in that seat too. I help you get there prepared and confident, even though I'm not in the room with you.

Services

What support looks like

Meeting preparation & coaching

Walk in knowing what to expect, what to ask, and what to listen for.

IEP document review

A clear read on goals, present levels, and services — in plain language.

Behavior plan guidance

Understanding BIPs and FBAs, and whether they actually fit your child.

IEP goal review

A clear look at your child's goals and data — what's being tracked, what progress actually looks like, and whether the goals are built to work.

Parental rights guidance

Understanding what you're entitled to ask for — and how to ask for it.

Before & after support

Strategy going in, and a debrief after — so nothing gets lost between meetings.
